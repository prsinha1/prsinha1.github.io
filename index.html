Priyanka Sinha

Postdoctoral Researcher in Mathematics and Statistics
University of Maryland, Baltimore County

My research studies learning and optimization under evolving data distributions, representations, and collaboration patterns. It examines how feedback, heterogeneity, and incomplete observations shape model stability, information preservation, and adaptive decision making, drawing on statistical machine learning, optimization, generative modeling, and game theory.

Research interests

  • Learning under feedback and evolving distributions
  • Generative models and longitudinal representations
  • Adaptive data selection and distributed optimization
