Researcher in mathematics and machine learning

Priyanka Sinha

Postdoctoral Researcher in Mathematics and Statistics

University of Maryland, Baltimore County

I develop methods for learning and optimization in systems whose data distributions, representations, and collaboration patterns evolve over time. My research studies how feedback, heterogeneity, and incomplete observations affect model stability, information preservation, and adaptive decision making. I use tools from statistical machine learning, optimization, generative modeling, and game theory.

Research areas

Three connected directions

01

Learning under feedback and evolving distributions

I study how performance feedback can guide models, routing, and collaboration as the data distribution changes.

02

Generative models and longitudinal representations

I use generative representations to isolate temporal change and study when rare structure is retained or lost.

03

Adaptive data selection and distributed optimization

I design methods for selecting informative data and coordinating learning when observations are incomplete and unequal.
